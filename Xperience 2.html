Featured Artists

KHAEDA
Live Performance
KHAEDA

KHAEDA (also known as Lord Khae) is a rising South African singer, songwriter, performing artist, and vocal coach. Renowned for her genre-bending sound, she seamlessly weaves raw, emotional storytelling across Amapiano, house, and alternative electronic music.

ORIIGINELLE
Live Performance
ORIIGINELLE

Innovative sounds blending Afrobeat with contemporary hip-hop. A unique voice in SA music.

TSHEGO SANTANA
Live Performance
TSHEGO SANTANA

Tshego Santana (born Tshegofatso Motlhabane) is an independent South African rapper, songwriter, and musical storyteller. Hailing from Zeerust in Lehurutshe, North West, he has established a highly distinct, alternative lane within the South African hip-hop industry through a fusion of introspective rap, melodic soul, Afrobeat, and dancehall elements.
